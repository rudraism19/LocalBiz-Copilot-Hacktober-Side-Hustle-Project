## Editor & Animator Technical Specifications

- **Editing Software:** Premiere Pro / DaVinci Resolve / After Effects / Final Cut Pro.
- **Color Grade:** Kodak 2383 warm film print emulation with lifted blacks (`#1C1917`) and saturated honey-amber highlights (`#EA580C`).
- **Motion Graphic Easing:** Cubic-bezier `(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` for snappy, organic UI card reveals.
- **Font Stack:** Inter Bold for UI headlines, JetBrains Mono for metrics and terminal code.
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 (1920x1080 or 3840x2160) for horizontal video; crop center 9:16 for TikTok/Shorts/Reels cuts.
